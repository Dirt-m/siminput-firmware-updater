Third-party files in this folder
================================

The Configurator copies these onto a box's RPI-RP2 drive when it puts the box
back on the stock SIMINPUT firmware. They are redistributed unmodified.

flash_nuke.uf2
--------------
Erases the RP2040's flash. Built from the Raspberry Pi pico-examples
(flash/nuke), https://github.com/raspberrypi/pico-examples. This is the
RP2040 build MobiFlight also ships as reset.raspberry_pico_flash_nuke.uf2.
